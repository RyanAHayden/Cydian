Paid tweaks in sideloaded apps: sign in to Chariz or Havoc and buy, or get free purchased tweaks without signing in
Home warns in red when signing needs setup or the Signing Server is down; a Why iFizBox tile
A source that fails to refresh opens a page with the whole error and Refresh / Edit / Remove; BigBoss loads again
Pull to refresh holds open while it refreshes; notifications show iFizBox's icon; the launch screen matches the app

- **Paid tweaks without a jailbreak**: a paid tweak in a Tweak Source signs in to its seller and offers Sign In / Buy for its price, as the jailbreak app does. A free tweak the seller already calls purchased downloads without signing in, in both apps. Your iFizBox Account carries your repo sign-ins between devices.
- **Add Tweaks**: Search All Repos finds tweaks for the app in repos you haven't added. The Add Tweaks page now slides in with a back arrow, and libraries like Comet are no longer offered as tweaks.
- **Home**: a solid red Signing Needs Setup tile (or Signing Server Down) takes the certificate tile's place until signing works. A Why iFizBox tile on the site, and in the app until you open it (then it moves to Settings > About). Home and Guides links to Sources or Search slide in, and pushed Sources and Changes keep their Refresh button.
- **Sources**: a failed source opens its page with the whole error and Refresh, Edit, Copy, Share and Remove. Packages.bz2 and .zst repos (BigBoss) read again. Sources > Add no longer asks to Allow Paste: Paste appears only when you copied a URL.
- **Feel**: pull to refresh holds the list open with a spinner while it works, with a haptic tick when it arms. The switch matches iOS 6's. Footer hints follow iOS 6's Settings style throughout. A fresh install starts in Automatic mode with the Gunmetal dark theme. Faster startup: other tabs load when you first open them.
- **Push** is on by default: iFizBox asks iOS once at launch.
- **Fixes**: notifications show iFizBox's icon instead of a blank one; the launch screen matches the first frame; self-update posts no notification; the sideload queue moves on once iOS has the whole IPA; share sheets close again on Cancel; paid packages show their price when iOS won't give the UDID; tweak pages in the sideload app say Needs Jailbreak instead of Not Your iPhone; source banners open only packages that loaded. Automatic installs take upgrades from https sources only. Credits > Artwork no longer lists a Pairing Screenshots row.

Sideload (no jailbreak): iFizBox.ipa, or add https://ifizbox.com/repo/source.json in the app or AltStore.
Jailbroken (rootless): iFizBox.deb, or the built-in https://ifizbox.com/repo/ source.
